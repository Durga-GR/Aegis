    If you're being threatened or blackmailed

    • Don't pay, and don't send more content -- it rarely stops the threats.
    • Stop replying, but don't delete the conversation -- it's evidence.
    • Take screenshots including usernames, phone numbers, and timestamps.

    How to report

    • File a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in (Report Cyber Crime > Women/Child related crime).
    • Or call the national cybercrime helpline: 1930.
    • Women's helpline: 1091. Emergency: 112.

    Relevant Indian law (plain language)

    • IT Act Sections 67 / 67A -- publishing or transmitting sexually explicit material electronically is a punishable offence.
    • BNS Section 356 -- covers defamation, which can apply when someone threatens your reputation.
    This guide is not a replacement for the police or a lawyer. If you are in immediate danger, please contact local emergency services or a trusted person right away.
